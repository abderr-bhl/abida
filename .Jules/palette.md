## 2025-05-14 - [Accessibility] Brand color contrast awareness
**Learning:** The Amazon brand orange (#FF9900) has insufficient contrast (approx 2.3:1) when paired with white text, failing WCAG AA (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text).
**Action:** Use dark text (like gray-900) on the Amazon orange background to ensure accessibility for all users, including those with visual impairments.
