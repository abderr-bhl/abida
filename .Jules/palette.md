## 2025-05-14 - Amazon Brand Contrast & External Links
**Learning:** The standard Amazon brand orange (#FF9900) has a low contrast ratio (~2.3:1) with white text, failing WCAG AA standards. Using dark text (e.g., text-gray-900) achieves a much better contrast ratio (~7:1). Additionally, links opening in new tabs must be explicitly indicated via icons and ARIA labels for better UX and accessibility.
**Action:** Always use dark text on Amazon-colored buttons and include an external link icon with an appropriate `aria-label`.
